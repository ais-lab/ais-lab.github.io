We recently had the pleasure of hosting a lecture by Dr. Philipp Müller from the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS).
In his talk, Dr. Müller introduced cutting-edge research on human behavior analysis from the perspectives of both psychology and artificial intelligence, providing valuable insights for all participants.
We would like to express our sincere gratitude to Dr. Müller for his inspiring and informative presentation.
