We participated in the 38th JSME Conference on Robotics and Mechatronics (ROBOMECH2026)!
Five members of the Lee Lab presented their research findings :microphone::sparkles:
It was a valuable opportunity to gain inspiration and learn a great deal through our presentations and interactions with researchers from other universities!

In between conference sessions, we also fully enjoyed Fukuoka's local cuisine and sightseeing :ramen::izakaya_lantern:
It was a memorable trip to Fukuoka that was fulfilling in terms of both research and leisure!

We look forward to applying the experience we gained here to our future research activities :fire:
