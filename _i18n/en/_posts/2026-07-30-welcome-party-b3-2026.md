We recently held a welcome party for the third-year students who will be joining the AIS Lab in the second semester of the 2026 academic year!
While enjoying pizza and sushi together, the new members had a great opportunity to get to know one another and interact with senior students. Welcome to the Lee Laboratory! We look forward to working together and wish you a rewarding and enjoyable time in the lab.
