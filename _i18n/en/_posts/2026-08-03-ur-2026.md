We participated in The 23rd International Conference on Ubiquitous Robots (UR 2026)!
A total of 17 members from the Lee Laboratory took part in the conference, supporting the event through conference operations and research presentations.
Researchers from Japan and around the world gathered to present a wide range of studies on ubiquitous robots and engage in active discussions. This conference was especially valuable for us because we were involved not only as participants, but also as members of the organizing team. It gave us a deeper appreciation of how an international conference is made possible through the cooperation of many people.
Through the research presentations and exchanges with international researchers, we gained valuable knowledge and inspiration. We would like to thank everyone who participated in and contributed to the successful organization of the conference.
