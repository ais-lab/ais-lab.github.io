As part of the official UR2026 Technical Tour, we hosted a lab tour and research demonstration!
During the tour, we introduced our laboratory facilities and research activities to UR2026 participants and demonstrated our research systems in action.
It was a valuable opportunity to share our research with researchers from around the world and exchange ideas with them.
Thank you very much to everyone who joined us!
