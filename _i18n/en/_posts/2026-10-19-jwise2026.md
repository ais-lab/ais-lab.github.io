Six students from our lab participated in the International Joint Workshop on Information Science and Engineering 2026 (JWISE2026), held in Seoul, South Korea, and presented their research findings.
Students from Ritsumeikan University and Dalian University of Technology presented their research, providing an opportunity to learn about each other's work and deepen mutual understanding.
